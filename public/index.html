    Frequently Asked Questions

    Quick answers about my design process, tools and how we can work together.

    01What is your design process?

    I begin by understanding the brief and objectives, followed by research, concept development, visual exploration, design execution, and refinement based on feedback.

    02Which software do you use?

    My primary tools include Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator. I also use Figma and AI-powered tools depending on the project.

    03Do you work with freelance clients?

    Yes, I'm open to discussing freelance projects, creative collaborations, and design requirements. Share your brief through my contact section to get started.

    04How long does a design project take?

    Timelines depend on the project's scope, complexity, and number of deliverables. I'll discuss the expected timeline after understanding your requirements.

    05Can you create designs according to brand guidelines?

    Yes. I can work with existing brand guidelines, including typography, color palettes, logo usage, and visual consistency, to create cohesive designs.

    06How can we work together?

    Use the contact section to share your project details, goals, and timeline. I'll review the brief and discuss the next steps with you.
